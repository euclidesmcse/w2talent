The W2 Profile is a standard card for each candidate. Every candidate shows up in the same format, so recruiters compare quickly.

## What is in it

- **Header:** first name, role, years of experience, city and work model, contract type and expected pay, and when they can start.
- **Summary:** up to 3 sentences written by AI from the profile.
- **Highlights:** up to 3 strengths, also written by AI.
- **Skills and languages:** taken from the profile the candidate checked.

## What is not in it

AI neither receives nor writes the full name, email or phone. Contact details stay protected (see [My data and privacy](/ajuda/privacidade)).

Candidates can create the W2 Profile again after changing their profile. Learn more in [How we use AI](/ia#ficha-w2).

## Automatic translation

For recruiters using the site in another language, the AI translates the title, summary, highlights and job titles of the published W2 Profile. Names, companies, cities, numbers and skills stay as they are, and the name, email and phone never go to the AI. Recruiters see the note "Automatic translation" and can open the original. Candidates keep seeing their profile as they wrote it. If the profile changes, the translation is redone.
