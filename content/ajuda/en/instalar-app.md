W2Talent works in the browser on computers and phones, with the same account.

## Install as an app

When the browser allows it, an "Install app" button shows at the top. It adds an icon to your home screen, and the site opens like an app.

On iPhone, use Safari's share button and "Add to Home Screen".

## Without internet

- Pages you already opened (Jobs, My profile, My applications) show the last copy, with its date.
- A notice says you are offline.
- Sending, publishing and new searches wait for the connection to come back.
