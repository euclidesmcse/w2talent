In [My profile](/minha-ficha) you turn your CV into a profile companies read in seconds.

## The three steps

1. **Send your CV** as PDF or Word (.docx), up to 5 MB, or paste the text. AI reads the text and fills in the fields. Your email and phone are read by rules and removed from the text before it goes to AI.
2. **Check the fields.** Fix what the reading got wrong. The profile needs the required fields; the [dashboard](/painel) shows what is missing.
3. **Create the W2 Profile.** AI writes a short summary and up to 3 highlights. Then you publish the profile with your candidate account.

## Jobs: type, duties and skills

Each job can have its **type** (full-time, part-time, project, freelance or internship), **what you did** (up to 5 short lines) and the **skills used**. The AI fills them in when it reads your résumé, and you check them. Up to 20 jobs fit, including ones held at the same time. Recruiters see all of this on a timeline of your career.

## Show the full résumé (optional)

In the last step, you can tick **Show my full résumé to recruiters**. W2Talent keeps the text of your résumé **without email, phone, links, documents and address** and shows it to signed-in recruiters who open your profile, with an AI-written summary. If you untick it and publish again, the text is deleted.

## Expected pay

Mark the contract types you accept (employee, contractor, internship...) and give the **minimum** you accept for each one.

## Limits

- Up to 10 CV readings and 20 W2 Profiles per day.
- The draft stays in this browser until you publish.
