The authenticator is the second step of sign-in: besides Google, Microsoft or your password, you type a 6-digit code from an app on your phone.

- **Recruiter:** required. Without it, recruiter pages are blocked, and it cannot be turned off.
- **Candidate:** optional, but recommended.

## How to turn it on

1. Open [Account security](/conta/seguranca).
2. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or another).
3. Type the code shown in the app.
4. Keep the 10 recovery codes somewhere safe. Each one works once.

## Day to day

After you type the code, this device is remembered for 30 days. On another device, or after that, the code is asked again.

## I lost my phone

Use one of the recovery codes instead of the app code. Then turn on the authenticator again on the new phone.
