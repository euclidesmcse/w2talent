In [Jobs](/vagas) you type the search the way you talk and press Enter or Search.

## Examples

- "remote DevOps contractor 15k"
- "mid-level data analyst São Paulo"
- "senior React employee"

## What W2Talent understands

Below the box, chips show what was understood: skills, contract type, work model, level and pay range. Click the × on a chip to remove it.

Skills look for jobs that ask for any of them. Contract type, work model, level and pay range do not hide jobs: they count in the [match](/ajuda/match).

## Areas

The pills below the search (Cloud, Data, Front-end...) bring the most requested skills of each area. Selecting one writes its name in the search.

## Similar jobs

When you open a job, up to 5 similar jobs show at the end of the details.
