You sign in with a Google or Microsoft account (personal or work) or with email and password.

## Email and password

In **Create account** you enter your name, email and a password of at least 10 characters. Passwords that already showed up in data breaches are refused. We send a link to confirm your email; it is valid for 1 hour and you can only sign in after using it.

Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in screen and create a new one from the email link. People who sign in with Google or Microsoft can also create a password this way. Changing or creating the password signs the account out of other devices.

After 10 wrong passwords in a row, password sign-in is locked until you create a new one through Forgot password.

If the email of the password account is the same as your Google or Microsoft account, both become one, as long as the email is confirmed.

## Browsing jobs needs no sign-in

Anyone can see and search jobs. Sign-in is needed to mark a job, publish your profile, send applications or search candidates.

## Candidate or recruiter

On your first visit you choose the account's profile, only once:

- **Candidate:** builds a profile, approves and sends applications. When you choose it, you accept the terms of use and data terms.
- **Recruiter:** registers the company, posts jobs and searches candidates. Needs the [authenticator](/ajuda/autenticador).

An account is either a candidate or a recruiter, not both. To use both sides, use another account (another email).

If you sign in with Google and later with Microsoft using the same email, both become the same account.
