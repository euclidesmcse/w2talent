The [Tech job market](/mercado) page shows numbers taken from the jobs and profiles published on W2Talent. It's open to everyone, no sign-in needed.

## Where the numbers come from

- Only from W2Talent's own active jobs and active profiles. No AI and no outside data.
- The calculation runs once a day; the page shows the week of the numbers.
- Example jobs and profiles are left out.

## How to read the tables

- **Salary range in jobs:** the posted "from" and "up to", by level and employment type, as a monthly amount. The **median** is the middle value; the **25%–75%** column shows where half of the jobs fall.
- **Candidate salary expectations:** the monthly minimum asked in profiles, by level and employment type. Profiles don't split expectations by work model.
- **Most requested:** skills with the most active jobs, how many profiles have each one and how many jobs there are per profile.
- **Trending:** jobs posted in the last 30 days versus the previous 30, only for skills with 5 or more recent jobs.
- **Time to first reply:** from the application until the company first changes its stage, across the site, in the last 90 days.

Pick a skill and, if you like, the work model (remote, hybrid or on-site) to see only its numbers.

## "Not enough data yet"

So that no one can be identified, a group only gets numbers with **10 or more** jobs or profiles and, when it comes from jobs, from **3 or more companies**. Below that the table says how many are missing, for example "4 more jobs needed". We never show the minimum or the maximum.

## Inside W2Talent

The same numbers show up in three screens, always under the rule above. The "job range" goes from the median "from" to the median "up to" of the group's jobs (same skill, level, employment type and currency).

- **On your [dashboard](/painel) (candidate):** "Your salary expectation and the market" compares the monthly expectation in your published profile with the job range for your first skill that already has numbers, at your level and employment type, and says whether it is below, within or above. It also lists up to 5 trending skills, marking the ones you already have.
- **In [Post a job](/publicar):** next to the suggested range, the job range and the candidates' expectations for the job's first skill, level, employment type and currency. Expectations are always aggregated from 10 or more profiles, never anyone's own. It is for information only.
- **On the [HR dashboard](/rh):** each active job with a pay range gets a "below", "within" or "above market" tag, comparing the middle of its range with the job range of the same group. With no numbers for the group, nothing shows.
