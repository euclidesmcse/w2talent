[My applications](/minhas-candidaturas) lists each job you sent, with the date.

## Statuses

- **Sent:** the application reached the company.
- **Seen:** the company opened your profile.
- **Answered:** the company replied.
- **Archived:** the job or the application is no longer active.

## Protected contact

The company talks to you through a W2Talent address. Your email and phone only show to them after you reply.

The [dashboard](/painel) shows the same numbers in short.
