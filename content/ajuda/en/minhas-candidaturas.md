[My applications](/minhas-candidaturas) lists each job you sent, with the date.

## Statuses

- **Sent:** the application reached the company.
- **Seen:** the company opened your profile or started reviewing it.
- **Answered:** the company wants to move forward with you (interview or offer).
- **Closed:** the company is not moving forward with this application, or it is no longer active.

## Protected contact

The company talks to you through a W2Talent address. Your email and phone only show to them after you reply or when they invite you to an interview. In that case only that job's company sees your contact, and you get a notice by email.

The [dashboard](/painel) shows the same numbers in short.
