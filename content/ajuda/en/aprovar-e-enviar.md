On W2Talent nothing is sent without your approval, job by job.

## Step by step

1. In [Jobs](/vagas), mark the jobs you like. They go to your selection, in the bar at the bottom.
2. Open [Review and approve](/vagas/aprovar). Each job shows the match with your profile and what is missing.
3. Read and approve them one by one.
4. Send. Only approved jobs go, with your W2 Profile.

To send, you need to sign in as a candidate and have your profile published.

## Sending rules

- Up to 10 jobs per send and 30 applications in 24 hours.
- The same job is never sent twice.
- If the company changes the job after you approved it, the approval drops and the job comes back for you to check.
- Expired or archived jobs are not sent.

After sending, follow up in [My applications](/minhas-candidaturas).
