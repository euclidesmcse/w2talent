The [HR dashboard](/rh) shows your company's jobs and the applications each one received.

## Company numbers

- **Active jobs** and the ones that **expire within 3 days** (renew them before they expire).
- **Applications** received and how many are **in the New stage**.
- **Time to first reply:** the average time between an application arriving and you first changing its stage.
- **Reply rate:** how many applications have left New at least once.

The numbers cover only your company.

## Jobs

Each job shows its status, the total number of applications and the new ones. An active job with a pay range gets a "below", "within" or "above market" tag: the middle of its range against the job range for the same first skill, level, employment type and currency in the [Tech job market](/mercado). Without enough numbers for the group, no tag shows. "Matching candidates" searches for profiles close to the job, including people who have not applied yet.

## Applications for a job

"See applications" lists the people who applied, from best to lowest match with the job.

**Adjusted order:** after 30 decisions, the order takes into account who your company moves to Interview (or later) and who it marks as Not moving forward. Only the order changes: the match % is still the same calculation, and age, gender, photo, marital status or address never count. Above the list you see "Order adjusted to what your company picks"; "See default order" goes back to ordering by match %. The same applies in Candidates when a reference job is chosen. Details in [How we use AI](/ia#ranking).

- **Stage:** New, Reviewing, Interview, Offer, Hired or Not moving forward. Change it with one click; there is no form.
- **What the candidate sees:** "Seen" when you open the profile or move it to Reviewing, "Answered" from Interview on, and "Closed" for Not moving forward. Internal stages are not shown to them.
- **Contact:** when you move someone to Interview (or later), their email and phone show in the list, only for your company, and they get a notice by email. Once released, it stays released.
- **Book an interview:** once the contact is released, offer 3 to 8 times (30, 45 or 60 minutes) and, if you want, the meeting link. The candidate picks one through the e-mail link and both get the calendar invite (.ics). They can change or cancel up to 12 hours before. Changing the times cancels the previous offer.
- **Compare:** tick up to 10 people to compare them side by side and export.
