W2Talent connects tech candidates with jobs. Candidates find jobs, check each one and send in a batch only the ones they approved. Recruiters get the W2 Profile, a standard summary of the candidate with the match explained.

## For candidates

- Using W2Talent is free.
- You build your profile from your CV in [My profile](/minha-ficha).
- You search the way you talk, for example "remote DevOps contractor 15k".
- Only what you approved goes to the company.

## For recruiters

- You register the company in [My company](/empresa).
- You post a job by pasting its text, and AI fills in the fields.
- You search candidates and sort them by match with one of your jobs.

This is the test version. The site changes often.
