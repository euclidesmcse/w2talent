W2Talent connects tech candidates with jobs. Candidates find jobs, check each one and send in a batch only the ones they approved. Recruiters get the W2 Profile, a standard summary of the candidate with the match explained.

## For candidates

- Using W2Talent is free.
- You build your profile from your CV in [My profile](/minha-ficha).
- You search the way you talk, for example "remote DevOps contractor 15k".
- Only what you approved goes to the company.

## For recruiters

- You register the company in [My company](/empresa).
- You post a job by pasting its text, and AI fills in the fields.
- You search candidates and sort them by match with one of your jobs.

## Talk to a person

Could not find the answer in Help? In [Talk to a person](/ajuda/conversa), you write to the W2Talent team, signed in or not.

- Not signed in, you give an e-mail. Signed in, we use the account e-mail.
- The reply shows up in the conversation and we let you know by e-mail, with the link.
- Keep the link: it is how you get back to the conversation. Anyone with the link can see the conversation.
- A person replies. Nothing in the conversation goes to AI.
- The team only sees what you wrote, the screen you came from and, if you are signed in, whether the account is a candidate or a recruiter.
- Limits: 3 new conversations per day and 30 messages per conversation per day, up to 2,000 characters each.
