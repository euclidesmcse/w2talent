## Protected contact

In candidate search, email and phone are masked (for example, a***@e***.com). The full contact only reaches the company after you reply to it.

## How long a profile stays in search

- A published profile stays in search for 180 days.
- Before it expires, you get email reminders with the "I'm still looking" button. Clicking it renews for 180 more days.
- Without renewal, the profile is archived and leaves search.
- After 30 days archived, it is deleted, together with its applications.

## AI and review by a person

AI helps read texts and write summaries, but it decides nothing for anyone. If an automated decision affected you, ask for a review by a person at contato@w2talent.com. See [How we use AI](/ia).

## When you sign out

Signing out deletes the drafts and selection saved in this browser.
