## Protected contact

In candidate search, email and phone are masked (for example, a***@e***.com). The full contact only reaches the company after you reply to it or when it invites you to an interview for a job you applied to (and you get a notice).

## The company's hiring system

Some companies turn on the integration with their hiring system (ATS). In that case, the application you send also goes to that system, with the same content you approved: the W2 Profile, without e-mail, phone or links. The system is also told when the company changes your application's stage, and your e-mail and phone only go there after the company invites you to an interview. There, the application follows the company's privacy rules.

## Salary expectation

The salary expectation you put in your profile shows to recruiters on your W2 Profile and in candidate search. In the market numbers ([Tech job market](/mercado) and Post a job), recruiters only see aggregated ranges from **10 or more profiles**, never anyone's own expectation, and never the minimum or the maximum.

## How long a profile stays in search

- A published profile stays in search for 180 days.
- Before it expires, you get email reminders with the "I'm still looking" button. Clicking it renews for 180 more days.
- Without renewal, the profile is archived and leaves search.
- After 30 days archived, it is deleted, together with its applications.

## Conversations with the team

Conversations in [Talk to a person](/ajuda/conversa) are kept until 180 days after they are closed; then they are deleted, with all their messages. Signed in, you see them in [My conversations](/ajuda/minhas-conversas). A person on the team replies, and nothing in the conversation goes to AI.

## WhatsApp notices

They are optional and start off. To turn them on, you confirm your mobile number with a code and accept an authorization, recorded with the date and the text. Each notice carries only the job title, the company name and a link to the website; never contacts, profile data or salary. The number is kept while notices are on and is deleted when you turn them off (with the button or by replying STOP). What you write to the W2Talent number is not stored or read by AI. Notices are sent through the official WhatsApp API, from Meta. See [WhatsApp notices](/ajuda/avisos-whatsapp).

## AI and review by a person

AI helps read texts and write summaries, but it decides nothing for anyone. If an automated decision affected you, ask for a review by a person at contato@w2talent.com. See [How we use AI](/ia).

## Training the AI with your corrections

It is optional and off by default. If you turn it on (on the W2 Profile, in Post a job or in [Account security](/conta/seguranca)), when you publish your W2 Profile or save a job the AI read, W2Talent keeps an example with what the AI wrote and what you corrected, to train and evaluate W2Talent's own open AI model.

- Before it is stored, names, emails, phone numbers, links, documents (CPF, CNPJ, ID numbers and others), addresses and company names are removed and become markers such as [NOME] and [EMPRESA]. If anything that looks like contact data is left, the example is not stored.
- The example does not carry your account id, only a code that lets us delete it.
- A person on the team reviews each example before it is used. Nothing is sent to outside training services without a new decision by the team.
- Turning it off deletes your stored examples right away.

## When you sign out

Signing out deletes the drafts and selection saved in this browser.
