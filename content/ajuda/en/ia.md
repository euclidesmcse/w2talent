AI helps with text tasks. It does not approve, reject or score anyone.

## Where it shows up

- Reading the CV and filling in the profile.
- Writing the summary and highlights of the W2 Profile.
- Reading the job text and filling in the fields.
- Rewriting the job without biased terms, when the recruiter asks.
- Suggesting interview questions.
- Answering questions in this help, only with what the articles say and always with the source link.

## Where it does not

The [match](/ajuda/match) is a calculation with fixed weights. Search uses text and meaning to sort, but does not hide candidates.

The conversation in [Talk to a person](/ajuda/conversa) is answered by a person on the team. Nothing in it goes to AI.

The full list, with the data each task uses, is in [How we use AI](/ia).

## A model of our own, in the future

If you want, your corrections can help train an open W2Talent AI model (see [My data and privacy](/ajuda/privacidade)). Training only happens with at least 500 examples reviewed by a person, and the trained model becomes one more option, compared with the current one first. Nothing switches by itself.
