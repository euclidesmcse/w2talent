AI helps with text tasks. It does not approve, reject or score anyone.

## Where it shows up

- Reading the CV and filling in the profile.
- Writing the summary and highlights of the W2 Profile.
- Reading the job text and filling in the fields.
- Rewriting the job without biased terms, when the recruiter asks.
- Suggesting interview questions.

## Where it does not

The [match](/ajuda/match) is a calculation with fixed weights. Search uses text and meaning to sort, but does not hide candidates.

The full list, with the data each task uses, is in [How we use AI](/ia).
