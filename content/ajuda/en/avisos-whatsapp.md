If you want, you get short notices about your applications on WhatsApp. It starts off. Emails always keep coming, with or without WhatsApp.

## How to turn it on

1. Open [Account security](/conta/seguranca) and go to "WhatsApp notices". You need to have created your profile.
2. Type your mobile number with WhatsApp, with the area code. Outside Brazil, start with + and the country code.
3. You get a 6-digit code on WhatsApp. It is valid for 10 minutes and allows 5 attempts. You can ask for up to 3 codes a day.
4. Type the code, read and tick the authorization, and confirm.

Notices only go to the number you confirmed here. Changing the phone in your profile does not change where notices go.

## What you get

- A company viewed your application.
- Your application changed stage.
- News about an interview (times offered or interview cancelled by the company).
- A job you approved, and have not sent yet, expires soon.
- The team replied to your conversation in [Talk to a person](/ajuda/conversa).

Each notice carries only the job title and the company name (or nothing, for the team's reply) and a link to the website. Contacts, profile data, salary or the text of conversations never go.

## When it arrives

- At most 3 notices a day.
- Only from 8am to 8pm, Brasília time. Anything that happens at night arrives at 8am.
- Several notices of the same kind on the same day become one.

## How to stop

- In [Account security](/conta/seguranca), tap "Turn off WhatsApp notices", or
- reply **STOP** on WhatsApp (SAIR, PARAR or SALIR also work).

It turns off right away: notices still waiting are not sent. To turn it on again, ask for a new code.

## Messages you send

This number only sends notices. Nobody reads what you write there, and no AI answers: any message other than STOP gets an automatic reply (one a day) pointing to the website. The text you send is not stored. To talk to the team, use [Talk to a person](/ajuda/conversa).
